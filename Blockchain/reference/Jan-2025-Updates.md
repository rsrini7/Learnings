### BNY Mellon digital cash & tokenized deposits – Jan 8–11, 2026

* **BNY Mellon announced a launch of on-chain, mirrored representations of client demand deposits (“tokenized deposits”) on its Digital Assets platform**, initially operating on a permissioned/controlled ledger; the bank says core legal/record-keeping remains on traditional systems while the tokens provide programmable on-chain cash for institutional workflows. ([BNY][1])
* The product is explicitly positioned to support **collateral, margin and near-real-time settlement use cases** for institutional clients, as part of BNY’s broader tokenization roadmap (and follows prior money-market/tokenized-fund initiatives among large custodians). ([BNY][1])
* Coverage and market write-ups characterize the move as an important bridge between conventional banking rails and tokenized money-market / stablecoin ecosystems given BNY’s custodian role. See Bloomberg, PYMNTS and other reporting for summaries and context. ([Bloomberg.com][2])

### CLARITY Act on digital assets – Jan 15, 2026 (scheduled)

* **The Senate Banking Committee scheduled an executive session / markup for H.R.3633 (the “Digital Asset Market Clarity (CLARITY) Act”) on Thursday, 15 January 2026 at 10:00 AM in Dirksen Room 538**. This is the committee calendar/listing published by the Senate. ([Senate Banking Committee][3])
* News and crypto-policy outlets report the January 15 hearing as the key next decision point where provisions and market-structure jurisdictional language (SEC vs CFTC) can be amended or advanced — markets are watching this as a major regulatory milestone for U.S. digital-asset market structure. ([CCN.com][4])

### X.com smart cashtags & crypto rails – Jan 10–11, 2026

* **X’s head of product (Nikita Bier) publicly teased “Smart Cashtags” that let authors specify exact assets (including crypto tokens / smart contracts) so a cashtag links to a precise on-platform asset page (prices, charts, posts)**; the original posts/teasers are on X. ([X (formerly Twitter)][5])
* Reporting (CoinDesk and other outlets) describes the feature as **cashtag + smart-contract awareness** that can disambiguate tickers and pull on-chain/market data — a capability that could position X as a financial UX layer (and potentially a front-end for payments/trading), though formal crypto payment rails were not announced. ([CoinDesk][6])

## Weekly Crypto & Macro Update – Week of 12–18 Jan 2026 (short synthesis)

* **Near-term risk:** Elevated volatility expected around the Dec-2025 CPI release (Jan 13, 8:30 AM ET), the Jan 15 Senate Banking CLARITY Act markup, and large token unlocks (notably ONDO mid-Jan). Markets priced for sensitivity to those three legs of risk. ([Bureau of Labor Statistics][7])
* **Institutional adoption signal:** BNY Mellon’s tokenized deposit announcement (permissioned, mirrored tokens) is a high-profile example of custodians bridging bank deposits and on-chain representations; view this as infrastructure-first (collateral/margin) rather than an immediate retail rails rollout. ([BNY][1])
* **Platform UX / distribution:** X’s Smart Cashtags point to mainstream UX changes that nudge users toward asset discovery and on-chain references inside social apps — important for distribution and education, though payments rails remain speculative so far. ([X (formerly Twitter)][5])
