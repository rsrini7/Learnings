## 🔬 Research & Breakthroughs

### DeepSeek MHC (Manifold-Constrained Hyperconnections) (Jan 2026)
- **Challenge**: Traditional residual connections create bottlenecks
- **Solution**: Mathematically constrain multiple information streams using Sinkhorn-Knopp algorithm
- **Results**: 27B model improved GSM8K from 46.7% → 53.8%
- **Efficiency**: 400% expansion in data flow with only 6.7% training time increase
- **Implication**: Foundational architectural innovation for R2/V4 models

### MIT RLM (Recursive Language Model) (Jan 2026)
- **Extends GPT-5 context**: From 16-33K tokens (failing) to 1M+ tokens
- **Hybrid approach**: LLM generates Python orchestration; deterministic execution handles loops; recursive sub-instances process text
- **Performance**: 32K-context tasks improved from 0.04% accuracy ($0.16) to 58% accuracy ($0.33) = **1000× improvement**
- **Architecture**: Treats long prompts as external files (like OS virtual memory)
- **Key insight**: Attention mechanisms alone insufficient; needs symbolic logic structures

### Correlation vs Causality – AI Limitation (Jan 2026)
**Einstein's 1953 insight**: Causality (not just pattern recognition) enabled Western science

**2025 Research findings**:
- Apple's "Illusion of Thinking": Model accuracy collapses on complex reasoning despite longer "thinking traces"
- Scientific discovery study: Models pass surface benchmarks but fail at iterative reasoning, hypothesis refinement
- **Thesis**: "Correlation can predict; it cannot explain"
- **AI limitation**: Current LLMs are pattern-matchers lacking causal models, logical reasoning, counterfactual testing

### New Moore's Law: 2× Every 4 Months (Jan 9, 2026)
- AI models' coding task complexity doubling every 4 months (accelerated from every 7 months in 2019-2025)
- Original Moore's Law (1975): chip capacity doubled every 2 years
- Recent acceleration reflects frontier models handling longer, more complex reasoning tasks
