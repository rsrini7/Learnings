## 🔬 Research & Science

### Yann LeCun's Paper — Ditching AGI for SAI (Late Feb 2026)
- Proposes **Superhuman Adaptable Intelligence (SAI)** over AGI framing
- Humans aren't general geniuses — we're specialized; AGI framing fuels hype and doomerism
- SAI focuses on measurable wins: adaptation speed via self-supervised learning
- Encourages diverse AI paths beyond GPT monoculture

### Ilya Sutskever's SSI — Biologically-Inspired Learning
- Safe Superintelligence (SSI) exploring alternatives to backpropagation
- Backpropagation is biologically unrealistic — uses global error signal, not local learning
- Promising alternative: **prospective configuration / predictive coding**
  - Network first "imagines" an ideal state, then reconciles predictions with input
  - Prevents catastrophic interference (new learning destroying old memory)
- Currently computationally expensive on GPUs; could define next-gen AI
- **Reference:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6vrLB-G7XZc
