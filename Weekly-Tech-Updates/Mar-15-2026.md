### Databricks KARL - Knowledge Agents via Reinforcement Learning
**Date**: March 13, 2026

Databricks unveiled **KARL**, a practical agent framework using reinforcement learning instead of static skills.

**Innovation**:
- **Synthetic problem generation**: System creates training problems from documents
- **Trajectory recording**: Agent searches documents, successful paths recorded
- **Weight updates**: Only best trajectories update model weights—baking search intelligence into neural network
- **Parallel verification**: Multiple "rollouts" for best aggregated answers
- **Performance**: Outperforms Claude 4.6 and GPT-5.2 on knowledge-retrieval tasks while being cheaper & faster
- **Distillation**: Intelligence transfers to smaller "student" models

## 🏭 Hardware & Infrastructure

### Intel 18A Manufacturing Process Reaches Production
**Date**: March 13, 2026

**Intel 18A** achieved production-viable defect density and entered high-volume production.

**Timeline**:
- 2021: Announced "5 nodes in 4 years" roadmap with RibbonFET, PowerVia
- 2025: Achieved production-viable defect density
- **January 2026**: High-volume production begins; Core Ultra Series 3 mobile CPUs announced
- **Q1-2026**: Systems shipping with 18A processors

**Technology**:
- **18A = 1.8nm effective process**
- **RibbonFET**: Gate surrounds channel on all sides (vs. FinFET); reduces leaks, heat, enables smaller size
- **PowerVia**: Backside power delivery reduces power loss by 30%, enables higher transistor density
- **Competitive timeline**: TSMC/Samsung expect similar tech late 2026 or 2027

**Products**: Acer, Dell, Samsung releasing 18A laptops; targeting handheld gaming with 70% performance boost
