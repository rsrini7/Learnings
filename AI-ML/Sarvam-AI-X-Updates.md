Republic x Sarvam AI Create History with Real-Time Translation of Nirmala Sitharaman's Budget Speech

The video highlights the historic collaboration between Republic Media Network and Sarvam AI to provide real-time AI-powered live translation and dubbing of the Finance Minister's budget speech .

Here's a breakdown of the AI-related content:

Real-time AI Translation and Dubbing :

Republic Bharat and Republic Kannada channels delivered the speech in Hindi and Kannada with the Finance Minister's own voice and intonations, with a latency of just 1.5 to 2 minutes .
This innovation allowed millions of viewers to access the budget speech directly in their own language without delays or subtitles .
Impact and Accessibility of AI :

AI was used to make information and intelligence accessible to all, bridging language barriers .
Feedback indicated that for the first time, people understood the budget end-to-end because it was presented in Hindi .
This initiative marks India's journey to build its own AI for its own needs .
Challenges and Execution of AI System :

The project involved capturing the right translation style, managing latency during live broadcasts, ensuring no glitches and clear audio, and handling technical issues .
It required strong coordination between different teams .
Collaboration and Future Possibilities of AI :

The collaboration with Sarvam AI opens up a world of immense possibilities that cut across languages, regions, and different voices .
Behind the Scenes of AI Model Building :

The AI models were built in India by a dedicated team .
The Republic team provided the platform for this innovation to be deployed for things that matter .
AI Model Testing and Accuracy :

The team constantly monitored logs to ensure no issues and had fallbacks in place for unexpected situations .
AI Model Training and Voice Mimicry :

The AI models are trained to mimic the voice, speaker similarity, and tone of the speaker .
These are voice cloning TTS models that can do zero-shot voice cloning in real-time for any speaker across multiple Indian languages .
The models are trained on large amounts of data, including different speaking styles, to mimic voice nuances in real-time .
They are also trained to understand the contextual nature of the translation, maintaining the formal tone of a speech video .
The models were specifically trained on the Finance Minister's voice to maintain speaker similarity .
AI for Democratizing Information :

AI can make information more accessible, allowing people to participate in what is happening around them in more accessible forms .
Language should not be a barrier, and AI helps eliminate the bottleneck of translating English content into other languages .
AI empowers democracy by making information available across languages .
Future Applications of AI in Content :

The technology can be applied to educational content, entertainment, and news and media industries to make content available in various languages while maintaining the speaker's identity .
AI System Building and Challenges :

Building the AI model was intense, with efforts focused on making it faster and more accurate while capturing the nuances of the speech style .
The team was nervous about potential mispronunciations or glitches, but the overall execution was smooth .
